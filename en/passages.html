Passages

A passage begins not with departure but with planning.

For ADVENTURE HORIZONS a passage is not a romantic line on the map. It’s a route calculation, fuel, ETA, weather, crew fatigue, fallback ports, and decision points.

Passage PlanningCroatia · Italy · Sicily · Balearics · Turkey

Basic logic

  • The route is calculated before leaving the marina, not as a scenic line.
  • Each long leg has fallback ports and departure scenarios.
  • Decision points are predefined and checked against the log.

Methodology

Weather, fuel, fatigue — three constraints of one decision.

A good passage is built on a conservative model: what if wind weakens, waves grow, engine runs longer, and skipper tires earlier.

Fuel

1 NM = 1 liter

For EVA we use a conservative rule: 1 nautical mile equals 1 liter of fuel. It gives a simple, strict reserve model.

ETA

Estimated Time of Arrival

ETA is not a pretty number but a working tool: daylight, marina, fuel, skipper fatigue and fallback plan.

Decisions

Fuel Decision Point

At the decision point we compare plan, log, fuel consumption, weather, and crew state. Then the route continues or changes.
